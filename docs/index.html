§1 — HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, and two of them are yours.

01 · YOU

Plug in your iPhone

Use a cable, unlock the phone, and tap Trust the first time.

02 · CAPSTAND

The screen appears

A frameless window fades in at the size and place you left it. Rotate the phone and the window turns too.

03 · YOU

Record it

Use QuickTime, OBS or any screen recorder. The phone's sound plays through your Mac.

§2 — SPECIFICATION

Small on purpose.

FRAMESDress the screenA drawn iPhone frame in Black, Natural, White or Desert Titanium, with or without the Dynamic Island. Four classic iPhone frames are built in, or you can use your own PNG. Rounded corners or no frame at all also work.
SIZEAny size, never distortedDrag any edge, or choose Small, Medium, Large, Fill Screen Height or Actual Pixels. Size and position are remembered for each phone.
LAYERFront, normal or behindKeep it above everything, treat it as a normal window, or tuck it behind all your other windows.
HOTKEYShow and hide from anywhere⌃ ⌥ ⌘ I hides the window and brings it back instantly. It needs no Accessibility permission.
SOUNDHear the phoneThe phone's audio plays through your Mac while its screen is showing, so your recorder can capture it too.
UPDATESKeeps itself currentCapstand checks for new versions and installs them when you say so. It never interrupts while your phone screen is showing.
BUILDNative and signedA universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, notarised by Apple. It lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon.
§3 — NOTES

Worth knowing before you start.

Why it asks for camera access

macOS treats a connected iPhone's screen as a camera, the same way QuickTime does. Capstand only shows the picture on your Mac. It never saves or sends it anywhere.

"Allow accessory to connect?"

Newer Macs ask this every time a device is plugged in. To stop the question, set Privacy & Security → Allow accessories to connect to Automatically when unlocked.

What leaves your Mac

One request to GitHub for the latest version number, a few times a day. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What it isn't

It's display only, by design, so you can't control the phone from the Mac. It needs a cable because the screen comes over USB.
